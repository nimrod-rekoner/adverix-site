FAQ

Questions we get asked

Which platforms do you work with?
WordPress and WooCommerce, Shopify and Webflow, or custom code when a project needs it. We pick what fits the business.
Where are you based?
We work remotely with clients across the US and beyond, with calls in your time zone.
How long does a new site take?
It depends on size and complexity. You get a clear timeline in the proposal before anything starts.
Can you take over a site someone else built?
In most cases, yes. We start with a backup and a review, clean up what is needed and improve speed and SEO from there.
What is GEO?
Optimizing so AI assistants like ChatGPT and Gemini understand and recommend your business. We combine it with classic SEO.
Will I be able to edit the site myself?
Yes. We build so you can change text, images and buttons easily, and train you before launch. If you prefer, we handle it on a monthly plan.
Do you offer monthly management plans?
Yes, plans for every scope, all with a direct line to our team.
Do you write content too?
Yes: website copy, product descriptions and SEO articles.
