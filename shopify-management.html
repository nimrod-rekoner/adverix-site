Why a store needs a manager

Shopify is great. It is also easy to get lost in.

Thousands of apps, themes that need careful tuning, and payments that have to be connected correctly. A store that sells needs a team that knows all of it.

We run Shopify stores day to day, including fashion and jewelry brands with pre-orders, coming-soon flows and international shipping.

FAQ

Questions & answers

My store is already on Shopify. How do you take over?
You add us as a collaborator from your admin. No passwords are shared and you can remove access at any time.
Which payment options can you set up?
Shopify Payments, Stripe, PayPal, Apple Pay and Shop Pay, depending on your needs. We connect them and test the full checkout.
Can my store sell in more than one language or currency?
Yes. With Shopify Markets we set up translations, local currencies and per-country settings, and check that checkout works in each market.
My store is slow because of apps. Can you help?
Yes. We audit every app, remove what is not needed and replace heavy ones with lighter options where possible.
What is GEO for a store?
Making your products and brand easy for AI assistants to understand, so they recommend you when people ask what to buy.
How much does Shopify management cost?
It depends on the size of the catalog and the monthly workload. You get a fixed price after the free check.
