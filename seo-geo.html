People do not scroll results anymore. They ask.

FAQ

Questions & answers

What is the difference between SEO and GEO?
SEO gets you ranked in Google results. GEO gets you cited and recommended inside AI answers. They share most of the groundwork.
How long until I see results?
Usually first movement within a few months, and real results over six months and more. SEO compounds over time.
Can you guarantee first place on Google?
No one honest can. What we promise is solid work, clear reporting and a plan that improves your position.
Why not just pay for ads?
Ads stop when you stop paying. Organic traffic keeps coming. The best plans usually use both, and we run ads too.
How do I show up in ChatGPT answers?
Clear content about what you do and for whom, structured data, consistent business details across the web and mentions on trusted sites.
Is this right for a local business?
Very much. Local SEO is often the fastest win for businesses that serve a specific area.
