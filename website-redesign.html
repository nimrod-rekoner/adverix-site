FAQ

Questions & answers

Why renew instead of building from scratch?
Because you keep your content, your URLs and the Google rankings you already earned, usually for less time and money.
Will my content be kept?
Yes. Everything you choose to keep moves to the new site.
Will it hurt my Google rankings?
Done properly, no. We keep URLs or set up redirects, and a faster site usually ranks better.
My site is built with Elementor and heavy. Can it be improved?
Yes, either by optimizing what is there or by rebuilding it lean. The audit tells us which.
What do I need to do?
Share what you want to change, send any new content and approve the design. We handle the rest.
Do you renew online stores too?
Yes, including product pages, checkout and payments.
