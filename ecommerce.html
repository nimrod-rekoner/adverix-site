FAQ

Questions & answers

Shopify or WooCommerce?
For most brands we recommend Shopify: reliable, fast and easy to run. WooCommerce makes sense when you need deep customization or already live in WordPress.
Can you migrate my existing store?
Yes. Products, customers, orders and redirects, so you keep your SEO.
Do you write product copy and take photos?
We write and optimize copy. For photography we work with your photographer or recommend one.
What happens after launch?
You can run the store yourself, or move to a monthly Shopify management plan with us.
How much does a store cost?
It depends on catalog size, custom features and integrations. You get a fixed quote after a short call.
