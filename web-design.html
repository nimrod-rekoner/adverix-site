FAQ

Questions & answers

Which platforms do you build on?
WordPress, Shopify and Webflow, plus custom code when a project needs it. We recommend the one that fits your business, not the one that is easiest for us.
Do you build on Wix or Squarespace?
No. We build on WordPress, Shopify and Webflow, or custom code, because they give you full ownership, speed and room to grow. If your site is on Wix or Squarespace today, we can migrate it for you.
What happens if something breaks after launch?
Tell us and we will look at it. The exact support period is written into your quote.
Is the site mobile friendly?
Always. Most visitors arrive on mobile, and Google favors mobile-friendly sites.
Can I get ongoing maintenance after the build?
Yes. Most clients move to a monthly management plan after launch.
Do you also do SEO, ads or content?
Yes. SEO + GEO, Google and Meta ads and content can all be part of the project.
How long does it take?
It depends on scope. You get a clear timeline in the quote, before anything starts.
