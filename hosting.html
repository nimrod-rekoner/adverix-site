Why hosting matters

Hosting is the foundation of your site.

Hosting affects load speed, visitor experience, data security and your Google ranking. Weak hosting means a slow site, crashes under load and more exposure to attacks.

FAQ

Questions & answers

What does the hosting include?
Fast servers, daily backups, SSL, security, monitoring, updates, a monthly form check and personal support.
How much does it cost?
It depends on the size of the site and the plan. Ask us and you get a clear price.
