FAQ

Questions & answers

How much does website management cost?
It depends on the size of the site and how much ongoing work it needs. After the free check you get a fixed monthly price, so you always know what you pay.
My site is slow and built with Elementor. Can you help?
Usually yes. Most slow Elementor sites improve a lot after we clean up plugins and code, compress images and set up caching properly.
What is GEO and why does it matter?
GEO is optimizing so that AI assistants like ChatGPT and Gemini understand your business and recommend it. More and more people ask AI instead of searching, so we work on it together with classic SEO.
You did not build my site. Can you still manage it?
Yes. Most sites we manage were built by someone else. We start with a full backup and a review, then take it from there.
Which platforms do you work with?
WordPress and WooCommerce, Shopify and Webflow.
How fast do you reply?
Usually the same business day, directly from our team.
