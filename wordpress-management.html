Why WordPress needs a manager

Flexible, powerful and easy to break.

WordPress is the most flexible platform there is, and it needs a steady hand: constant updates, plugins that clash, login attacks and sites that slow down over time.

We manage all of it so you do not have to think about any of it. And when you need an answer, you have a direct line to our team on WhatsApp.

FAQ

Questions & answers

My Elementor site is heavy. Can you speed it up?
In most cases, yes. The usual culprits are too many plugins, uncompressed images and missing caching, and we can fix all three.
Can plugin updates break my site?
They can, which is why we back up before every update and check the site after. If something breaks, we roll it back right away.
My site was hacked. What now?
Contact us right away. We clean it, close the hole that let the attacker in and harden the site so it does not happen again.
I have a WooCommerce store. Is that included?
Yes. Store management can be part of the monthly plan, depending on how much ongoing work it needs.
What is GEO for a WordPress site?
Making your content and structure clear enough that AI assistants can understand and recommend your business.
How much does it cost?
It depends on the scope. After the free check you get a fixed monthly price.
