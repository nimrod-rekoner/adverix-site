Outgrown your spreadsheets or an old database? We build custom tools like CRMs, client portals and dashboards, and automate the repetitive work around them. Practical systems that fit your business, not the other way around.

The idea

Start with the task you hate most.

Most businesses lose hours to copy-paste work: moving leads between systems, answering the same questions, updating stock by hand. We find those tasks and automate them, one at a time, so you see value fast.

When off-the-shelf tools stop fitting, we build a proper web app around your process. Nimrod, our founder, maps the work with you personally before anything is built.

How it works

From idea to working system.

Step 01

Map the work

We list the repetitive tasks, the tools you use and how much time it all takes.

Step 02

Pick the first win

The task or tool with the best time-saved to effort ratio.

Step 03

Build & test

Built on tools you already use where possible, or as a custom web app when they are not enough. Tested on real data.

Step 04

Hand over & improve

A short guide for your team, then the next improvement.

FAQ

Questions & answers

Do I need new software?
Not always. We build on the tools you already use when they fit, and build custom only when it is worth it.
Can you replace our spreadsheets or old database?
Yes. We move your data into a proper web app with the screens and permissions your team needs.
Will AI talk to my customers without control?
No. Any customer-facing AI is limited to your approved content and hands over to you when it is not sure.
How much does it cost?
Each project gets a fixed price after we map the work.
What if something stops working?
We set up alerts for failures, and maintenance can be part of a monthly plan.
